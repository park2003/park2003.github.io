node 00
manuscript

Quantum Cramér-Rao bounds for squeezed Fock states under decoherence

J. Park and S. Mouradian, in preparation. Derivation draft dated 2 Nov 2026.

Plain version: a weak force nudges a trapped ion's motion. We read the nudge through the ion's internal qubit. I derived how small a nudge you can resolve when the motion starts in a Fock state |n⟩ with squeezing r, and how cooling, heating and dephasing during the measurement change which state to use.

resultBelow a crossover noise rate, higher Fock states win; above it they lose, and every state falls onto one universal line. For 6.98 ms of exposure the crossovers are 31, 10 and 6 s⁻¹ for n = 1, 2, 3.

node 01
protocol

The sensing protocol in phase space

Prepare the motion, let the signal displace it by β, split the two spin branches by ±α with a spin-dependent force, measure the qubit. The whole measurement reduces to one number, the qubit coherence C = V e−iΦ: Φ carries the signal, V is how much the branches still overlap.

node 02
derivation

Closed forms for the bound

  • 02.1Ground state: bound e4α²/16α², minimum e/4 at α = ½.
  • 02.2Squeezed Fock state: overlap V = e−2α²e−2rLn(4α²e−2r). Each quantum adds a node; squeezing rescales α by e−r.
  • 02.3Optimal readout solves Ln − yLn + 2yL′n = 0, independent of r. For n = 1, y = 2 + √3.
  • 02.4Cold bath during the drive: factor (2/Γt)(1 − e−Γt/2) and penalty e4α²(1−e−Γt); thermal bath multiplies the penalty by 2n̄ + 1.
  • 02.5Near zero noise the best bound grows as esΓt, s = (2n̄+1)e2ryn − ½, which gives the crossover rates.

Which state to prepare, at which noise level

The best achievable bound over all readouts, as a function of noise. Higher n starts lower and climbs faster. Crosses mark the overtakes. Squeezing helps the ground state most and the higher Fock states least once dephasing is strong, because dephasing removes exactly the number-basis coherences that give |n⟩ its edge.
